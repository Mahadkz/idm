XDM Browser Integration

Private Window Interception

By default, private and incognito window downloads are kept inside your browser to protect session privacy.

To allow private interception, toggle "Allow in incognito / private windows" in your browser's extension management page.

Site Rules & Exclusions

Domains excluded from interception bypass XDM and download directly through your browser.

  • *.bank.example
  • *.internal.local

Force / Bypass Keys

Hold these modifier keys while clicking a link. Force sends the link to XDM even when it is excluded or interception is off. Bypass lets the browser download it.

Combine alt, ctrl, shift and meta with "+". The two combinations must differ.

Download Links Filter

Applies to "Download selected links" and "Download all links".

Media Panel & Player Overlay

Minimum size applies only when the total size is known (Content-Length or Content-Range). Media of unknown size, and streams, are never hidden. 0 means no minimum.

The overlay is never shown for DRM-protected (EME) media.

Mistaken Captures

When you cancel captured downloads from one site repeatedly, XDM offers (never forces) to exclude that site.

Repair & Native Host Status

If downloads fail to hand off to the XDM desktop application, ensure the XDM daemon is running and the native messaging manifest org.xdm.browser is registered.
